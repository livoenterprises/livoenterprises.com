SMALL PROMPT FOR AN AI GUIDE (2026-11-12)
The AI reads a short starter prompt (about 200 words), then opens the index and only the topic files it needs from livoenterprises.com.
A topic file is 4-10 KB; the whole pack is 109 KB. The full pack stays as the fallback for assistants that cannot open web pages.

Needs, published first: the universal-links website zip and the go-links guide zip (the topic files carry /go/ links; the build checks keys against go-keys.json).

1. Copy this zip's site/ contents into  Livo Content Edits/site/ . It adds ai/ (32 files), llms.txt, and updates tools/build_pack.py, pack/pack.txt,
   pack/00-instructions.md, livo-guide-pack.md and _headers. _headers here is the FINAL version: it has the security block, the
   apple-app-site-association stanza from the universal-links zip, and a text/plain stanza for /ai/ and /llms.txt. It replaces the earlier _headers.
2. LivoContent.sh publish "Small prompt: llms.txt and ai/ topic files"
3. Check: open https://livoenterprises.com/llms.txt and https://livoenterprises.com/ai/curator.md in Safari (plain text).
4. Try it: paste Livo_Starter_Prompt.txt into an AI that can browse, then ask "Where do I fix Live Photo links?" Worked = it opens llms.txt and curator.md and
   answers Organize > Curator > Clean up and review > Fix Live Photo Links, with the /go/ link. Didn't = it says it cannot open pages (use the full pack),
   or it answers without opening anything (tell me which assistant).
5. After any guide edit, rebuild:  python3 site/tools/build_pack.py  (from the Livo Content Edits folder), then publish. It rewrites the pack, ai/ and llms.txt.
