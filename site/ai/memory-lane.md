# Memory Lane

*Guide topic 11 · version 5 · updated 2026-11-10 · https://livoenterprises.com/guide/memory-lane.html*

Memory Lane is where what you have made lives, and the quickest way to browse everyone and everywhere you have named.

## People, Places and Events

Along the top of [Memory Lane](https://livoenterprises.com/go/memory-lane) are three rows: **People**, **Places** and **Events**, everything you have named, with a photo count on each. Tap one to see its photos in the Gallery. **See all** opens the full list.

## Live and Sealed

**Live** shows your Memories, Scrapbook pages and Collage pages as cards: the date, the first lines of the story, the hero photo. Double-tap a card to open it; swipe left for **Rename**, **Combine with…** and Delete (Delete moves it to the Trash). Press and hold a card for **Make a Gift…**. A sort button switches between oldest first, the default, and newest first, and Livo remembers your choice. **Combine with…** adds another Memory's photos to the end of this one, skipping any it already has; the other Memory is not changed or deleted. A gold **Sealed** mark means a Time Capsule holds this Memory. **Sealed** lists the capsules themselves; tap one to look inside, read-only.

## Opening a Memory

The review screen shows the story in full, with **Edit**, **Play** for the narration, and the **Share** menu. Swipe left or right to move between Memories; pull down to close. The "Sealed in" chips open the capsule that holds it.

## Share

- **Share as Video**: the Memory as a plain video file, for anyone; see [Making a Memory](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/making-a-memory.html).
- **Make a Gift…**: a dedication, the video made for you, and a sealed gift with every way to deliver it; see [Making a gift](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/making-a-gift.html).
- **Share with Livo**: the Memory's Time Capsule, for someone who has Livo. If the Memory is not sealed yet, Livo asks **Seal It First?**, seals it, and then opens the share sheet.

## Order prints

The book button on the top row (beside New Memory) opens **Order prints** (part of Livo Plus): a book made from any Memories, Scrapbook pages and Collage pages you choose, in your order, as a PDF for a print shop or your own printer. See [Order prints](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/order-prints.html).

## Filtering

The Gallery's Filter also narrows Memory Lane; the banner under the Live/Sealed row says what is on, and its ✕ clears it. A Memory that came from someone else's capsule is marked "From" and the sender's name.
