# Tag the Moment

*Guide topic 5 · version 1 · updated 2026-11-09 · https://livoenterprises.com/guide/tag-the-moment.html*

One Moment, one fact at a time. Pick the people, tap Apply, and the whole day is named.

## Opening it

[Open the Gallery](https://livoenterprises.com/go/gallery), pick a Moment, and tap the **tag icon** beside the date. The screen shows the Moment's title, a count ("12 photos · Moment 3 of 40"), arrows for the previous and next Moment, and four tabs: **Event**, **Kind**, **People** and **Place**. Only the open tab's field shows, so the photos keep the room. A dot on a tab means you have picked something for it.

## People

Tap who is in these photos. The names you use most are at the top; **Likely** lists the people already in this event and these days, so one tap each is usually enough; the person icon adds someone new. Then tap **Apply (12)**. Apply adds and never removes: a photo that already had a name keeps it.

To take a person off the photos they are not in: select those photos (tap them in the strip or the big picture) and tap the person's **minus chip** under "Take off the 3 selected". One tap, and the name leaves those photos only. **Same people as the photo above** copies one photo's people to the next, for a run of photos of the same group.

## Event and Place

On **Event**, type a few letters to find any event you have made, or pick **Last used**; a new name makes a new event. An event's dates grow as you add photos to it, so one event can cover a whole trip. On **Place**, type a few letters or pick from your Places. Apply writes the event and the place onto the selected photos. See [Events and the story](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/describing-a-moment.html) for the story that goes with an event.

## Kind

A Kind says what a photo is rather than who is in it: a selfie or a screenshot, so the Filter can hide them. Livo marks the ones it can tell on its own; this tab is for the rest. [Curator](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/curator.html) adds more Kinds.

## The strip and the big photo

A strip of small thumbnails runs above one large photo. Swipe the big photo left and right to move through the day in time order, or tap a thumbnail to jump. Each thumbnail is badged for the open tab: a filled dot when the photo has that fact, a dim ring when it does not, so what is still missing shows at a glance. **Tap** the big photo to include or skip it for Apply; **press and hold** opens it full screen. Beside it are that one photo's own pills; **×** takes one off at once, and **Edit** opens the full tag screen for that photo (event, kind, people, place, story and tags).

## Undo

Every Apply and every take-off can be taken back. **Undo** at the top undoes the last one; tap it again for the one before, as many times as you like in this Moment. Nothing leaves the phone and nothing is guessed: a name Livo does not know becomes a dashed chip that asks you.

## Moving on

The arrows go to the previous and next Moment, and the header resets for each one. The count at the top tells you how much of the library has been looked at.
