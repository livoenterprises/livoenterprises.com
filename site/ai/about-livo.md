# About Livo

*Version 5 · updated 2026-11-10*

Livo: short for life, and for living it well.

Every life is a story. Livo helps you tell that story and share it.

I chose it because it is short, and because it sounds like *life*. It made me think of living well, of a life well lived, with its ordinary mornings, its laughter, and the people we love. Then I understood what the name was for: holding on to those moments, and giving them to the ones who come after us.

*From Livo Enterprises: technology for living well.*

Livo Enterprises believes technology should serve life, not crowd it. So we hold ourselves to two goals. We **make life simpler**, so the things that matter are not buried under effort. And we **give people control**, so your memories stay yours, in your hands and never in anyone else's keeping. Livo is where that begins: the photos and videos of a lifetime, kept simply and kept safe.

A **LivoCapsule** is how a life is handed on. It gathers your photos, videos, names and stories and seals them into one gift: a video that plays with one tap, a page that opens in any web browser, and the files themselves with the names and stories written inside them. Years from now, a grandchild you may never meet can open it and hear the laughter, see the faces and learn the stories. A moment that is stored is only kept. A moment that is shared keeps living.

Livo is free, with no limits, and everything in that loop is in it: bring in your photos, say who is in them and what happened, make a Memory, seal a capsule, make a gift, open anything anyone sends you. **Livo Plus**, the one purchase, adds the printed forms of a gift (the book, the poster, prints with names) and **Curator**, for the person who keeps the family's archive: scanning prints, bringing photos in from OneDrive and many years of devices, sharing with the family through their own storage, and the deeper organizing tools. See [Free, and Livo Plus](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/livo-and-livo-plus.html).

## Two promises

1. It makes life simpler, not more complicated.
2. You stay in charge.

## What Livo is not

- Not a cloud: no Livo server, no Livo account, no password to lose.
- Not a photo-recognition app: Livo never guesses who someone is. It makes naming fast, by hand.
- Not a replacement for Photos: it adds to your library, and writes names and stories into exported files in fields other apps can read.
- Not a subscription: Livo is free, Livo Plus is one purchase, and nothing you have made is ever locked away.
