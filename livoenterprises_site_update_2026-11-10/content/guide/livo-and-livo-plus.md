## Free, forever

**Your photos, named.** Bringing in photos and videos from the Camera Roll and iCloud Photos, and the duplicate tidy-up. Moments, the Gallery, Filter, Livo Search (names, places, events, dates, words in photos). Tag the Moment (Event, People, Place), Name the Faces (with the photo peek, the tags tidy-up and recent names), Edit Tags, stories and tags on photos, Always offer. The People, Places and Events lists and editors, and the rows at the top of Memory Lane.

**Memories.** Making them, editing them, narrating them, Scrapbook pages and Collage pages, Save page to Photos, Share as Video. No limit on how many.

**Time Capsules.** Sealing, looking inside, Keep a copy in iCloud Drive or any folder in Files, the checks and repairs, Keepsakes, Bring in, Export, Share with Livo.

**Make a Gift.** The dedication, the video Livo makes for you (with a small "with Livo" line at the end), the whole sealed gift, the folder of photos for a frame or a TV, plain print-size photos, and sending by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files.

**Receiving, always.** Opening a capsule or a gift someone sent you, Watch, Add to My Livo, Save to Photos as an album. Nobody has to buy anything to receive what you made.

**Everything else.** Backup & Restore to this phone and to Files or iCloud Drive. Getting Started, this Guide, Settings and all five palettes. No account, no sign-in, no limit on photos, people, Memories, capsules or gifts.

## Livo Plus, one purchase

One purchase, made once, shared with your Apple family. It adds two kinds of thing.

**For giving:**

- The gift book: an 8 × 8 inch PDF with the dedication on the cover, a page per photo with the names, and the people at the back, plus the same pages as pictures for any print service.
- The collage poster, 16 × 20.
- Print-size photos with the names strip under each ("Anna and Laurie · November 2019").
- Order prints from Memory Lane: a book from any Memories, Scrapbook pages and Collage pages, in your order.
- Give it again, from a gift capsule: the whole "Your gift is ready" screen for a gift you already made.
- Gift videos without the "with Livo" line.

**For the family's archive (Curator):**

- Scan Prints.
- OneDrive: photos from OneDrive folders, backups copied to OneDrive, a copy of every capsule in your Livo Vault folder, Send to a Livo.
- Family Share and the Family Library, Ask Family, Save to Livo.
- The Collections surface; Buckets, Life Events, the Tags list, saved and pinned searches, the full selection bar.
- Reference and Saved-images Kinds with text recognition; thumbs-down, Picks, To Review.
- Media Sources, Activity in full, Where my photos are, Send to Photos.

See [Curator](/guide/curator.html) for what each of those is.

## The rules, either way

- **Nothing already made is ever locked.** Without Plus, everything you have made stays yours to see, edit and export, and what the Curator tools made (Buckets, Life Events, OneDrive copies) stays readable with Curator off.
- **Tools, never a clock or a count.** No trial that expires, no limit that fills up. Plus unlocks tools, once.
- **One product.** Livo Plus is the only purchase. Curator is the name of its archive tools, and the switch for them is in Settings.
- **Restore Purchases** is under Settings › About, for a new phone or a family member's.

## Why it is drawn this way

Everything that makes a gift worth sending, and everything needed to open one, is free, because a gift that arrives is how Livo reaches the next family. The finished forms of a gift (a book in the hand, a poster on the wall) and the tools of the person who keeps thirty years of everyone's photos are where a one-time purchase is fair.
