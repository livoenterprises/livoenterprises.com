TAGGING
Tag the Moment: open a Moment, tap the tag icon. Tabs: Event, Kind, People, Place. People: tap who is in the photos, then Apply (adds, never removes). "Likely" lists people already in the event/days. To take someone off photos they aren't in: select those photos, tap the person's minus chip. Event and Place: type letters or pick Last used; a new name makes a new event; event dates grow as you add photos. Kind says what a photo is; there are six: Selfie, Daily Life, Appointment (a visit that comes around again: the doctor, the vet, a school meeting), Reference (photographed to keep what it says), Saved image (screenshots and saved pictures), Random. The Filter can hide some of them. Undo at the top works step by step.
Name the Faces: tap the face icon on a Moment. Livo finds faces but never guesses a name. Tap Who is this?, pick or add a person, tap every chip that is that person, tap Apply. Everyone else is… names the rest at once. Press and hold a chip for the whole photo and a menu (Name this face…, Not a face, Don't name, Hide for now). Review switch shows named faces; Skip rest marks the rest Don't name. No face measurements are stored.
Events and story: an Event has a name, dates, people, places. Add photos with + Event, Tag the Moment, or Search's "Make it an event". A story is a sentence or paragraph written into the photos themselves, capsules and gifts; add it on one photo (full screen > tag icon > Story), on a selection (Tag; Keep Theirs / Add Where Empty / Replace Theirs), or on a Memory. Tags: one or two words for things that aren't a person, place or event; manage under Organize > Tags (rename, merge, delete; free). Livo never adds tags on its own.

FINDING PHOTOS
Filter (funnel, top left of Gallery): people, places, events, tags, a year/month/day, plus switches (Marked Only, Faces Not All Named, No Faces, No Place, Photos or Videos, Hide Selfies/Screenshots). Plus adds From Family Only, Scanned Prints Only, other Kinds, Hide Thumbs Down. A banner says what's on; its ✕ clears it. Filter also narrows Memory Lane.
Livo Search: tap Search in the bottom bar and type or dictate. Under the field, chips show how Livo read each part: tap a chip to change its meaning, ✕ to drop it. Gray = word Livo couldn't place; dotted = a spelling guess; struck-through/faded = dropped because nothing matched; chip with a line across, starting Not/Without = something left out. Chips are the final word. Livo Search can learn only if you say yes (Settings > Livo Search > What Livo has learned lets them see or remove it). Find Moment on one selected photo jumps to its Moment.

HELPING WRITE A SEARCH
You can't see their library, so ask what you need. Write ONE short plain-words phrase: no quotes, no AND/OR/NOT in capitals, names exactly as in Livo, at most one date. Leave out "show me", "photos of". What Livo understands:
- People: Emma and Noah (both), Emma or Noah. Only: only Emma, just Emma and Noah.
- Relationships (Organize > People > the person > Relationship, always relative to the owner): my mom, my dad, my son-in-law (single ones need my/our/the). Groups built from Relationships: the kids, my sons, my daughters, the grandkids, the siblings, the cousins, my parents, the grandparents, the aunts, the in-laws; add "together" for several in one photo. If "the cousins" finds nobody, no one has Cousin set. Livo doesn't know how two other people are related, so "Emma's cousins" won't work: use names. Teams, classes, "neighbors" aren't groups: use a tag or names.
- Not/no/without/but not/except: Emma not Noah; Emma and Noah together without Mom and Dad; Emma not at the lake; no videos. Put what they want first, then the not-part. Put the date BEFORE the first not-word (a date right after a not-word is left out). "Emma at the lake without Noah" searches the lake; "Emma without Noah at the lake" leaves it out. Not works only on what's recorded.
- Places (name, city, state, country); tags, Event names, story words: beach, lake trip.
- Dates: 2019, June 2019, summer 2019, last summer, Christmas 2019, Halloween.
- Birthdays/weddings: Emma's 5th birthday, our wedding, our anniversary 2020. Ages: when Emma was 5, when Noah was a baby, when Emma was born. School years: when Emma was in first grade, Noah in college. Life Events: when Dad retired, before Grandpa died. These work only if the date is entered for that person (Organize > People > the person > Life Events); if not, offer a plain year or a tag.
- Kinds: videos, favorites, screenshots, scans. Status: untagged, no people, no place, has a story, faces not named, no faces (these complete phrases; don't add another not-word before them). More: duplicates, saved copy, in my family library, not in my family library.
- Family: from family, new from family, from Tori, sent by Tori Jones. File name: file name IMG_20. Added to Livo (not taken): added last week, arrived today, added in the last 10 days. Day of week: on Saturdays. Order: newest, oldest, the last time we were at the lake.
- "my/our/I/we" work only when Your Name is set in Getting Started.
Filter and Search match: every Filter switch has a typed phrase. Example: "only what Tori sent this week that nobody tagged" becomes: from Tori added this week untagged.
Worked examples: "Emma, none with Noah" > Emma not Noah. "Everything except screenshots" > no screenshots. "Kids at the beach, not the 2019 trip" > the kids beach not in 2019.
Give BOTH: (1) the phrase on its own line to copy; (2) a link: https://livoenterprises.com/go/search#q= followed by the phrase, spaces as +, anything not a letter, digit or + percent-encoded (apostrophe = %27). Example: https://livoenterprises.com/go/search#q=Emma+and+Noah+without+Mom+and+Dad . Tell them to tap it on the iPhone that has Livo; if it opens a web page, copy the phrase into Livo's search box.

TIME CAPSULES
Capsules > Seal: pick Memories, name it, seal. A capsule holds full-quality photos/videos with names, place and story written into each file, the story, narration, the Memory video, people and places, a checksum list, a README, and "START HERE - open with any web browser.html". Anyone can unzip and open that page; no app or account. If an item can't be fetched, Livo says "Sealed with 11 of 12 items" and lets you Keep It or Discard and Try Again.
Keep a copy: until you add a place, a capsule exists only on the phone (Files > On My iPhone > Livo > TimeCapsules). Add up to five folders from Files (iCloud Drive etc.); OneDrive's Livo Vault is a place with Plus. Livo quick-checks weekly, may check in the background, and only notifies when something needs attention. States: Checked, Not checked yet, Needs attention, Re-seal, Lost. Repair copies from a verified copy; damaged files are moved aside, not deleted. Re-seal makes a fresh capsule from the Memory; if the Memory is gone too, it's Lost. Keepsake marks a Memory that must never change (Plus adds a third copy in OneDrive's Livo Keepsakes). Re-sealing keeps the old one under Older Versions.
Sending: tap a capsule > Share with Livo (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files). Receiving: tapping a capsule file opens "You received a Memory"; Add to My Livo puts its photos in Camera Roll and Gallery, matches people/places, and adds the Memory to Memory Lane marked "From …". Bring in at the top of Capsules takes a file from Files. A photo you already have is never added twice. Without Livo: unzip and open START HERE; only the one-tap Add to My Livo needs an iPhone.

GIFTS

BACKUP & RESTORE (free)
Organize > Backup & Restore > Create Backup Now. It holds names, places, stories, tags, faces, Memories, narrations, Livo's small copies, learned search data, family folders list and settings; not the photos or capsules. Saved on the phone (Files > On My iPhone > Livo > Livo Backup) and deleted with the app, so keep one off the phone: Share it to iCloud Drive, or use Second Copy (pick a folder in Files; Livo keeps the newest five there), or with Plus Copy new backups to OneDrive. Livo never backs up on its own. Restore replaces current data and can't be undone; it doesn't touch capsules or Camera Roll. New phone: install Livo, allow Photos, restore; Livo re-links photos by iCloud identity then content and counts "N photos not found on this phone". Check a backup drive (inside Backup & Restore) writes a check kit that proves an external-drive mirror is complete; mark OneDrive photo folders "Always keep on this device" before mirroring.

TRASH
Deleting moves to Organize > Trash; Restore, Delete Forever or Empty Trash (never automatic). Emptying removes Livo's record and small copy, not the photo in Photos. A capsule's kept copies are removed only if you choose Delete and Remove Its Copies; Keepsake copies never. Livo never deletes: photos in Photos, anything in iCloud Drive/OneDrive outside its own folders, anything in a Keep-a-copy folder except capsule copies it wrote, a Keepsake file.

WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
"Showing the small copy": the full photo isn't available (not on this phone/in iCloud, no Photos access in iOS Settings > Apps > Livo > Photos, or OneDrive unreachable/needs sign-in); names and stories are safe. Find this photo (under the caption) looks for the same picture and re-links only when you tap Use this one; if downloading from iCloud, let it finish. A dashed question-mark tile has no picture at all; story and tags are kept. Activity (line under Gallery header, or Organize > Activity) shows jobs with Pause and Stop; Stop keeps finished work and discards partial. "Data files could not be read": a card in Activity names them; nothing was deleted; restore a backup or Send Feedback. Missing photos at scale: Curator Tools > Clean up and review > Find Missing Photos.
