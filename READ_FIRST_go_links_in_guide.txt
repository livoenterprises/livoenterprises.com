GO LINKS IN THE GUIDE AND THE PACK (2026-11-12)
Needs the universal-links zip published first (go/ pages and go-keys.json), so the keys can be checked.

What changes: guide masters stay as they are (livo:// links, which the app uses). When the website is built, each livo:// link
becomes https://livoenterprises.com/go/<key> (before, it was plain text). The pack does the same and tells the assistant it may
share those links. A livo:// key missing from go-keys.json stops the build with its name, so a bad link cannot go out.

Steps:
1. Copy this zip's site/ contents into Livo Content Edits/site/ (tools/build_content.py, tools/build_pack.py, pack/00-instructions.md,
   livo-guide-pack.md).
2. LivoContent.sh publish "Guide and pack: /go/ links"   (the guide pages rebuild with live links)
3. Test on the phone: open livoenterprises.com/guide/curator.html and tap a link in the page. With Livo and the capability set,
   it opens the screen; otherwise the /go/ page shows.
