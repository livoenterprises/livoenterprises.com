Technology for living well.

Livo is our word for life, and for living it well. We make technology that leaves your days simpler and more enjoyable, and that answers to you.

Most of us own more technology than ever and feel less in charge of it. Each new device and app arrives with its own menus to learn, its own account to keep up, and its own quiet appetite for information about us. Livo Enterprises exists to build the other kind: products that take work off your hands and leave the decisions in them.

Two promises

Every Livo product is held to both.

  1. It makes life simpler, not more complicated.

    A product earns its place by taking something off your plate. If it gives you more to manage than it removes, it isn’t finished.

  2. You run the technology. It doesn’t run you.

    In practice that comes down to two things.

    It learns how you work
    For decades people have been trained to accommodate machines: learn the menu, learn the gesture, learn the file format. That’s backwards. You should be able to say what you want in your own words, and the product should do the adapting.
    Your data is yours
    Whatever a product learns about you belongs to you. It stays on your devices, in formats you can open without us, and it goes to other people only when you decide, and only to the people you pick.

What that means in practice

  • No Livo account to create, and no Livo server holding your information.
  • No subscriptions. When you buy a Livo product, it’s yours.
  • Plain, documented file formats, so what you make outlives the app that made it.
  • Nothing acts on your behalf without showing you first and waiting for your yes.
