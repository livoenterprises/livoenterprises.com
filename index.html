Software for things a family keeps.

Your photos, your stories, your grandmother's voice telling them. Stored on your own devices, in a form anyone can open, for as long as anyone cares to.

Livo Enterprises is a small, independent software company. We make apps around one conviction: the things people record about their lives belong to them, not to the service that happened to hold them. A photo should still be a photo when the app that made it is gone. A story recorded for a grandchild should open on whatever a grandchild uses to open things in fifty years.

That means no accounts to maintain, no subscriptions to lapse, and no server between you and your own memories. It also means the formats we write are plain, documented and self-contained, so that what you keep stays yours.

LivoCapsule

Our first app, for iPhone and iPad. Choose the photos and videos that tell a story, add the story in your own words or your own voice, and seal them into a single file that opens in any web browser. Nothing to install on the other end.
