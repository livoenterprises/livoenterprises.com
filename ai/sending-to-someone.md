# Sending to someone

*Guide topic 13 · version 2 · updated 2026-11-09 · https://livoenterprises.com/guide/sending-to-someone.html*

A capsule you send is a file. The person opens it in Livo with one tap, or in any browser without Livo at all.

## Share with Livo

[Open Capsules](https://livoenterprises.com/go/vault), tap a capsule, then **Share with Livo**. The capsule goes into the share sheet: AirDrop when you are in the room, Messages or Mail when it is small enough, or Files for an iCloud Drive link to something bigger. From a Memory, **Share › Share with Livo** seals it first if it is not sealed yet. The file is named so that anyone can tell what to do with it: "Livo Capsule - Anna Lee - Friday Night - 2026-10-03 - UNZIP, then open START HERE.zip".

For a gift, with a dedication and a video that plays with one tap, see [Making a gift](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/making-a-gift.html). With [Curator](https://livoenterprises.com/guide/curator.html), a capsule can also go as a OneDrive link, or to the whole family at once through Family Share.

## Receiving a capsule

Tap a capsule file in Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files and Livo opens it: "You received a Memory" from the sender, with its name, what is in it and when it was sealed. Tap **Add to My Livo**. The capsule goes into your Capsules; its photos go into your Camera Roll and your Gallery, in the Moment of the day they were taken; the people and places it names are matched to yours or created; and the Memory appears in Memory Lane with "From Anna". **Not Now** changes nothing.

A photo you already have is recognized and never added twice, even if it arrives again in another capsule. **Stop** while adding keeps the photos already added and makes no Memory; the capsule's own screen carries on where it stopped.

## Receiving a gift

A gift opens with its dedication ("For Grandma · 80th birthday · from the Jones kids") and a **Watch** button that plays the video at once, before anything is added. **Add to My Livo** brings in the Memory as above; **Save to Photos as an album** puts the photos and the video into an album in Photos named for the gift, for someone who would rather stay in Photos. A line at the bottom says the gift was made with Livo, and how to make one.

## Without Livo

Unzip the file on any computer and open **START HERE - open with any web browser.html**. The photos, the story, the narration and the video are all there; a gift's page starts with the dedication and plays the video at the top. An Android phone or a Windows PC can play the video and see the photos; only the one-tap Add to My Livo needs an iPhone.
