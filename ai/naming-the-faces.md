# Naming the faces

*Guide topic 6 · version 4 · updated 2026-11-10 · https://livoenterprises.com/guide/naming-the-faces.html*

Livo finds the faces; you say who they are. It never guesses a name.

## Opening it

[Open the Gallery](https://livoenterprises.com/go/gallery), pick a Moment, and tap the **face icon** beside the date. Livo finds the faces in that Moment's photos (the first time takes a moment) and shows each one as a small chip, in the order the photos were taken.

## Name, then tap

1. Tap **Who is this?** and pick a person — type a name, say it, or choose from the list; **Add "…" as a new person** makes one on the spot. The band at the top of the list, **In this Moment, and recent**, holds the people already tagged on these photos and the people you have named in earlier Moments this session, so a name is one tap once you have used it.
2. Tap every chip that is that person. Chips from the same photo dim, because a person appears only once in a photo.
3. Tap **Apply**. Those chips leave the grid and the photos are tagged with that person.
4. Pick the next person. When everyone left is one person, **Everyone else is…** names them all at once.

**Press and hold a chip** to see the whole photo, large, with that face outlined, and a menu: **Name this face…**, **Not a face**, **Don't name** (a stranger), **Hide for now**, **Show the photo**. The chips are three across so each face is big enough to read. **Skip rest** marks everything left as Don't name, after asking, and goes on to the next Moment with faces to name. Faces you marked **Not a face** wait in a collapsed row at the bottom, "Not faces (3)"; open it and tap a chip, or its **Restore**, to put that face back as unnamed.

## Review

The **Review** switch at the top shows every named face in this Moment, grouped by name. Tap a face to rename it or un-name it. Previous and Next move through Moments that have named faces.

## Moving on

When a Moment is done, the card tells you how many Moments still have faces to name. **Next Moment** goes to the next one that does, and the Gallery behind moves with you. If photos in the Moment carry a person whose face is not among the named ones (you tagged the whole day generously in Tag the Moment, say), the card offers **Take them off** with the count and the names, and an Undo; it counts only photos where every face is named, never one with a face Livo found but you did not name.

## Where the names go

Naming a face tags the photo with that person, so Tag the Moment, the person's screen, the Filter and Livo Search all find it; Tag the Moment is the faster way when a Moment has no faces to find, or when you know who was there without looking. Removing a person from a photo also un-names their face there. No face measurements are stored and nothing leaves the phone; a Time Capsule carries the names, never the face boxes.
