## "Showing the small copy"

Open a photo full screen and, if the full-size picture cannot be shown, the caption under it says why: the photo is not in Photos on this phone (it was deleted, or is still in iCloud); Livo has no access to Photos (Settings › Apps › Livo › Photos); OneDrive cannot be reached, or needs you to sign in again. Livo shows its own small copy meanwhile; your names and story are safe either way.

## Find this photo

Under that caption, **Find this photo** looks in Photos for the same picture — by its fingerprint first, then among photos taken at the same moment — shows you what it found beside Livo's copy, and re-links it only when you tap **Use this one**. If the photo is still downloading from iCloud, let Photos finish and try again.

## A dashed square

A tile with a dashed question mark is a photo Livo has no picture for at all. Its story and tags are kept; open it and use Find this photo.

## Activity

[Organize › Activity](livo://organize/activity) shows everything Livo is doing in the background — an import, a backup, a share to the family, the Vault's copies — with progress, failures and Pause or Stop. The line under the Gallery header opens it. Pausing always says what happens next.

## "Data files could not be read"

If Livo cannot read one of its own data files, [Organize › Activity](livo://organize/activity) shows a card at the top naming them. Nothing was deleted. Restore a backup, or send the names with Send Feedback.

## Stop means stop

When you stop a job, what was finished stays and what was in progress is discarded; nothing half-done is kept.

## Reaching us

In Organize › Settings, scroll to the About section at the bottom and tap **Send Feedback**. It opens a mail to feedback@livoenterprises.com with the build and your phone's details already filled in. Say what you tapped and what happened; a screenshot helps. Or write to [support@livoenterprises.com](mailto:support@livoenterprises.com).
