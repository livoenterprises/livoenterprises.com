## Opening it

[Open the Gallery](livo://gallery), pick a Moment, and tap the **face icon** beside the date. Livo finds the faces in that Moment's photos (the first time takes a moment) and shows each one as a small chip, in the order the photos were taken.

## Name, then tap

1. Tap **Who is this?** and pick a person — type a name, say it, or choose from the list; **Add "…" as a new person** makes one on the spot.
2. Tap every chip that is that person. Chips from the same photo dim, because a person appears only once in a photo.
3. Tap **Apply**. Those chips leave the grid and the photos are tagged with that person.
4. Pick the next person. When everyone left is one person, **Everyone else is…** names them all at once.

Long-press a chip for **Not a face**, **Don't name** (a stranger), **Hide for now**, or **Show the photo**. **Skip rest** marks everything left as Don't name, after asking.

## Review

The **Review** switch at the top shows every named face in this Moment, grouped by name. Tap a face to rename it or un-name it. Previous and Next move through Moments that have named faces.

## Moving on

When a Moment is done, the card tells you how many Moments still have faces to name. **Next Moment** goes to the next one that does.

## Where the names go

Naming a face tags the photo with that person, so the person's screen, the Filter and Livo Search all find it. Removing a person from a photo also un-names their face there. No face measurements are stored and nothing leaves the phone; a Time Capsule carries the names, never the face boxes.
