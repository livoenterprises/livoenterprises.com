## Scanning

[Open the Gallery](livo://gallery), tap **+**, then **Scan Prints**. The camera is Apple's own document scanner: it finds the print's edges, shows the box, and captures when the print is lined up (or on your tap). Lay down the next print and it captures again. Tap **Save** when the batch is done — about twenty prints at a time is comfortable.

The first time, iOS asks for permission to use the camera.

## About These Prints

One sheet for the whole batch: a **date** for all of them (leave it blank and they are dated now), a **story**, **tags**, **people** and a **place**. Skip anything you do not know. Each print becomes a photo in your Camera Roll, dated as you chose, one second apart so the box's order is kept, and comes into Livo with those details.

## Scanned prints in Livo

Each one carries a small scan mark on its tile, "Scanned print" in Info with the time it was scanned, and the Filter has **Scanned Prints Only**. Change a print's date later with Set Date, as for any photo.

## The words on a print

As each print is added, Livo reads the printed and handwritten words on it — a caption on the back, a date in the corner, a letter — on the phone, with nothing sent anywhere. Info shows **Text in this print**, and Livo Search's **Text** field finds the print from a word on it. The tile shows **Aa** when words were found. Only scanned prints are read; no other photo is.
