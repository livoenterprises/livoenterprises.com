## Two steps

Deleting a photo, Memory, capsule, person or place moves it to [Organize › Trash](livo://organize/trash), where it waits until you **Restore** it or **Empty Trash** (or **Delete Forever** on one item). The Trash never empties itself.

## What Empty Trash removes

- A **photo**: Livo's record and its small copy. The photo in Photos or OneDrive is untouched, and Livo remembers not to import it again.
- A **Memory**: the story, order and narration.
- A **Time Capsule**: its record and its file on this phone (a Keepsake's copy in OneDrive is never removed).
- A **person** or **place**: the entry itself. Restoring a merged person or place undoes the merge.

The confirmation names the consequence for that kind of thing.

## What Livo never deletes

Your photos and videos in Photos. Anything in OneDrive outside Livo's own folders, or in another person's OneDrive. Anything in a Vault place you chose. A Keepsake file. A capsule file you opened from the Files app. Nothing is ever deleted on its own, on a schedule, or because space is low.
