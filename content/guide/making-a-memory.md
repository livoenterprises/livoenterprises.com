## From marked photos

Mark the photos you want (swipe up in the Gallery), then tap **New Memory** on the marked row, or **+** on [Memory Lane](livo://memory-lane). The canvas opens with your marked photos in the order they were taken; the first is the hero.

## The canvas

Write the **Story** (type or dictate). Long-press a photo to make it the hero; swipe a photo left to take it out; **Add from Library** brings more in. The **Memory Date** is the date the Memory is about — Livo sets it from the earliest photo, and you can change it. **Save** keeps it; a new Memory then asks whether to clear your marks.

If some photos are no longer on this phone, Save says so and offers **Find Them** (see [When something looks wrong](/guide/when-something-looks-wrong.html)) or **Save Anyway**, using Livo's small copies.

## Narration

On the Memory's screen, tap **Record** to tell the story in your own voice. Listen back, then **Keep**, **Record Again** or **Discard**. The recording is kept with the Memory and travels in its Time Capsule.

## Share as Video

**Share › Share as Video** renders the Memory as a video — the hero with the title as the first frame, each photo in turn, your narration over it — and offers the share sheet: Save Video, AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files, and **Share to Family**. The file is named after the Memory and the date, and it plays anywhere.

## Editing later

Every Memory stays editable: open it from Memory Lane, tap **Edit**. Editing never changes your marked photos. Putting the photos in a different order is coming.
