## Choosing a Moment

The Gallery opens empty. Tap the calendar at the top left to see your Moments, newest first, and pick one. The ‹ and › arrows beside the date move to the earlier and the later Moment. Tap **All Moments** to see the whole library at once (a big library takes a few seconds; the card says so); picking a Moment turns it off again.

## The Moment header

Beside the date are two small icons: the **face** opens Name the Faces for this Moment, and the **tag** opens Describe the Moment. See [Naming the faces](/guide/naming-the-faces.html) and [Describing a Moment](/guide/describing-a-moment.html).

## Marked photos

Swipe a photo up to mark it (thumbs up), down to hide it from Memories (thumbs down). The **N Marked** pill shows how many are marked across the whole library; tap it to see only them. Marked photos are the set a new Memory is made from — see [Making a Memory](/guide/making-a-memory.html).

## Selecting photos

Tap a photo to select it; keep dragging to select more. A row appears under the header: **Select All / Unselect All** (what is on screen), **Mark Selected** (thumbs up on the selection only), **Unmark All** (clears every mark in the whole library; nothing else changes), and **Share**. The bar at the bottom offers what you can do with the selection: Info and Find Moment (one photo), Tag, Rotate, Set Date, Stitch (videos), Move to Bucket, Delete, Make a Video.

## Opening a photo

Tap a selected photo, or double-tap any photo, to see it full screen. Swipe between photos, pinch to zoom, pull down to close. A LIVE pill plays a Live Photo. If the full-size picture cannot be shown, the caption under it says why — see [When something looks wrong](/guide/when-something-looks-wrong.html).

## The badges on a photo

A small mark in the corner tells you what a photo is: video, LIVE, stitched, used in a Memory, in a Bucket, a scanned print, or **Aa** when Livo has read text on it.
