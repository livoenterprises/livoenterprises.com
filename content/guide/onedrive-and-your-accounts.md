## Signing in

[Open Settings › Data › OneDrive Accounts](livo://settings/onedrive) and tap **Add Account**. Microsoft's own sign-in runs inside Livo; Livo never sees your password. You can add more than one account; one is **active for import**, and photos remember which account they came from. **Choose Folders** picks what to import; **Reset import progress** starts an import over.

## Needs sign-in

Microsoft asks for a new sign-in after a password change, after 90 days without use, or on a new phone. The account's row then reads **Needs sign-in** with a **Sign in again** button; the account, its photos and its folders are kept. "OneDrive can't be reached right now" means no network; Livo shows the small copies meanwhile.

## Livo's folders in your OneDrive

Livo makes these at the top of your OneDrive, only when you use the feature, and writes only inside them:

- **Livo Backup** — your backups; private.
- **Livo Vault** — a copy of every Time Capsule you seal.
- **Livo Keepsakes** — a copy of each Keepsake, written once and never deleted.
- **Livo Family Share** — what you share with the family; shared with them as view-only.
- **Livo Sent** — capsules you sent as a link.

None of them is ever imported as photos. Livo deletes inside them only what you ask it to (a backup, your Family Share files, a deleted capsule's Vault copy), and never in anyone else's OneDrive.
