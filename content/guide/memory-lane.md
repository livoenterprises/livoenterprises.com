## Live and Sealed

[Open Memory Lane](livo://memory-lane). **Live** shows your Memories as cards — the date, the first lines of the story, the hero photo. Double-tap a card to open it; swipe left to move it to the Trash. A gold **Sealed** mark means a Time Capsule holds this Memory. **Sealed** lists the capsules themselves; tap one to look inside, read-only.

## Opening a Memory

The review screen shows the story in full, with **Edit**, **Play** for the narration, and the **Share** menu. Swipe left or right to move between Memories; pull down to close. The "Sealed in" chips open the capsule that holds it.

## Share

- **Share as Video** — the Memory as a plain video file, for anyone (see [Making a Memory](/guide/making-a-memory.html)).
- **Share with Livo** — the Memory's Time Capsule, for someone who has Livo. If the Memory is not sealed yet, Livo asks **Seal It First?**, seals it into the Vault, and then opens the share sheet.

## Filtering

The Gallery's Filter also narrows Memory Lane — the banner under the Live/Sealed row says what is on; its ✕ clears it. **From Family Only** shows only Memories and capsules the family sent you; they carry "From <name>".
