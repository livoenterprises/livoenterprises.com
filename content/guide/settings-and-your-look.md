## Your name

[Settings › Your Name](livo://settings/your-name) is the name stamped on the Time Capsules you seal and signed on the stories you write. It also makes you a Person in your People list, marked "This is Me".

## Your look

[Settings › Appearance](livo://settings/appearance) offers three palettes, always dark with white lettering: **Charcoal** (the darkest, the default), **Slate**, and **Spring**, a soft teal for people who find the others too dark.

## Story Display

**Show Names Instead of "I"** changes how a story reads on screen only; the story you wrote is never rewritten.

## Data

**OneDrive Accounts** — see [OneDrive and your accounts](/guide/onedrive-and-your-accounts.html). Backup & Restore and Activity are under [Organize](livo://organize).

## About

[Settings › About](livo://settings/about): **About Livo** (what Livo is and the three promises), **Getting Started**, this guide, the Privacy Policy and Support, the version and build, and **Send Feedback**, which opens a mail to us with the build and your phone's details filled in.

## Livo Full

Livo is free for everything that keeps your family's knowledge safe: importing, naming, describing, searching, backups, receiving what the family sends, and the Vault's checks for every capsule you have. The one-time purchase **Livo Full** unlocks making beyond the first three Memories and three Time Capsules, and sharing to the family. Nothing you have made is ever locked away, and one purchase is shared with your Apple family. *(Coming in a later build; everything is unlocked today.)*
