## Your name

[Settings › Your Name](livo://settings/your-name) is the name stamped on the Time Capsules you seal and signed on the stories you write. It also makes you a Person in your People list, marked "This is Me".

## Your look

[Settings › Appearance](livo://settings/appearance) offers three palettes, always dark with white lettering: **Charcoal** (the darkest, the default), **Slate**, and **Spring**, a soft teal for people who find the others too dark.

## Story Display

**Show Names Instead of "I"** changes how a story reads on screen only; the story you wrote is never rewritten.

## Optional, or Data

If you have never signed in to Microsoft, this part of Settings is called **Optional** and holds one row, **Use OneDrive and Microsoft family sharing**. Livo works fully without a Microsoft account; tap the row only if you want OneDrive. After that the section is called **Data** and holds **OneDrive Accounts** — see [OneDrive and your accounts](/guide/onedrive-and-your-accounts.html). Backup & Restore and Activity are under [Organize](livo://organize).

## Livo Search

**What Livo has learned** lists what you have taught Livo Search: the meanings you confirmed ("the cabin" is one of your places), your recent searches, what you open and share (which only orders suggestions), the searches you run often, and names you chose not to add. Swipe a line to forget it, or Clear a list. Livo never learns from a guess.

## About

[Settings › About](livo://settings/about) is the last section of Settings: scroll to the bottom. It holds **About Livo** (what Livo is and the two promises), **Getting Started**, the Privacy Policy and Support, the version, the build and a Content line that names the version of each page on your phone, the **Livo Full** rows, and **Send Feedback**, which opens a mail to feedback@livoenterprises.com with the build and your phone's details filled in.

## Livo Full

Livo is free to use, and everything in it works: importing, naming, describing, searching, backups, capsules and their checks, sharing with the family, and OneDrive copies. A free Livo lets you **create five Memories** (a Memory, a Scrapbook or a Collage saved from the canvas), counted for life. Deleting one does not give the spot back, and Memories that came from your family, from a capsule or from a restore do not count. The one-time purchase **Livo Full** removes that limit.

Nothing you have made is ever locked away: everything already made stays viewable and editable. Settings › About shows your count ("Free Memories 2 of 5 created") and has **Restore Purchases**. One purchase is shared with your Apple family.
