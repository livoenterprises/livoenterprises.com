## Sealing

[Open the Vault](livo://vault) and tap **+** (**Create Time Capsules**). Pick the Memories; each one is sealed into its own capsule and appears in the list at once. The first time, Livo asks for your name, which is stamped on every capsule you seal. **Stop** keeps the capsules already finished and discards the one in progress.

## What is inside

The photos and videos at full quality (with the names, places and story written into each photo, where other apps can read them), the story, your narration, the Memory as a video, the names of the people and places, and a page called **START HERE - open with any web browser.html**. Unzip the capsule on any computer and open that page: no app, no account, nothing to install. The README inside says so, and asks whoever keeps it to copy it to a new drive every five years or so.

## Where capsules are kept

On this phone, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Livo › TimeCapsules; in your OneDrive's **Livo Vault** folder; and in any other place you choose. See [Keeping capsules safe](/guide/keeping-capsules-safe.html).

## The list

Each capsule shows its name, the date and time it was sealed, and its state: **Checked 6 Oct · 2 places** when all is well; **Not checked yet**; **Needs attention**; **Re-seal**; **Lost**. The line at the top sums them up. The **Filter** button shows only capsules from the family, or older versions. Tap a capsule for its screen: look inside, Export, Send to a Livo, the checks and repairs, **Keep forever (a Keepsake)**, and Delete.

## Importing

**Import** at the top takes a capsule file from Files. Its photos go into your Camera Roll and your Gallery, and the Memory appears in Memory Lane — see [Sending to someone](/guide/sending-to-someone.html).

## Older versions

Re-sealing a Memory makes a new capsule and keeps the old one under Older Versions; nothing you sealed is ever thrown away by Livo.
