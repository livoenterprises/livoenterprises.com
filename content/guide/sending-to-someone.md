## Send to a Livo

[Open the Vault](livo://vault), tap a capsule, then **Send to a Livo…**. **Send as a File** puts the capsule in the share sheet — AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files. **Send as a OneDrive Link** uploads it to your OneDrive's Livo Sent folder and shares a link with a short note on how to open it. For the whole family at once, use [Family Share](/guide/family-share.html).

**Export** (also on the capsule's screen) saves a copy named so that anyone can tell what to do with it: "Livo Capsule - Ken Jones - Friday Night - 2026-10-03 - UNZIP, then open START HERE.zip".

## Receiving a capsule

Tap a capsule file in Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files and Livo opens it: "Ken sent you a Memory", with its name, what is in it and when it was sealed. Tap **Add to My Livo**. The capsule goes into your Vault; its photos go into your Camera Roll and your Gallery, in the Moment of the day they were taken; the people and places it names are matched to yours or created; and the Memory appears in Memory Lane with "From Ken". **Not Now** changes nothing. **Keep this forever (a Keepsake)** is offered at the same time.

A photo you already have is recognised and never added twice, even if it arrives again in another capsule.

**Stop** while adding keeps the photos already added and makes no Memory; the capsule's own screen in the Vault carries on where it stopped (**Add to My Livo**).

## Without Livo

Unzip the file on any computer and open **START HERE - open with any web browser.html**. The photos, the story, the narration and the video are all there.
