## Two versions, one app

**Livo** is free, with no limits, and it is the whole loop: bring in your Camera Roll, name the people, name the event and add a line of story, make a Memory, seal a capsule, pass it on, make a gift. It works with your Camera Roll and iCloud, keeps copies in iCloud Drive and Files, backs up and restores, receives what your family shares with you, and sends by AirDrop, Messages and Mail. Nothing to sign up for, and no limit on how much you make.

**Curator Tools** is for the person who maintains the family's photos: who has the shoebox of prints to scan, who has photos in many formats from many years, cameras, phones, drives, cards and cloud accounts, and who is trying to bring all of it into one place, name it, and make it available to the family for generations. Curator Tools is the set of tools for that. It is part of **Livo Plus**, the one purchase, shared with your Apple family, never a subscription; see [Free, and Livo Plus](/guide/livo-and-livo-plus.html) for the whole plan, including what Plus adds for giving.

## What Curator Tools adds

- **Scan Prints**: printed photos in with the camera, a box at a time, with the words on them read for search. See [Scan Prints](/guide/scan-prints.html).
- **OneDrive**: photos from OneDrive folders, backups copied off the phone, a second copy of every capsule in your own OneDrive, and capsules sent as links. See [OneDrive and your accounts](/guide/onedrive-and-your-accounts.html).
- **Family Share** setup: sharing your own photos with the family from your own OneDrive. Seeing and receiving what the family shares is free, in Organize › Family. See [Family Share](/guide/family-share.html).
- **Buckets** under Organize: a shelf for photos you want out of sight but not gone (a burst's thirty near-identical shots, the photos from a past relationship you are not ready to see every day). Moving photos into a Bucket is part of Livo Plus; opening a Bucket and taking photos out stays free. See [People, Places, Life Events, Tags and Buckets](/guide/people-places-and-more.html).
- **More Kinds**: Reference and Saved images beside Selfie, Screenshot and Scan, with text recognition on them and **Read text again** in a photo's Info.
- **More marks**: thumbs-down and Hide Thumbs Down, Picks, To Review.
- **Deeper search**: the longer grammar (commas, "and", the Story pill).
- **Clean up and review**: Duplicate Check, Fix Dates from File Names, Earliest Dates, the Life Events clean-up, Find Missing Photos, Fix Live Photo Links and Read Apple’s Keywords and Ratings.
- **Housekeeping**: Media Sources, Where my photos are, Send to Photos, Check a backup drive.
- The **full selection bar** in the Gallery, with every action a tap away.

## Nothing is hidden or locked

Curator Tools never hides or locks anything you made. Without Livo Plus, a Bucket still opens and photos still come out of it, OneDrive photos already brought in are still in the Gallery, and capsules already copied to OneDrive are still copied. Without Plus, a tap on Curator Tools shows what Plus is and what stays free, never an error, and changes nothing.

## Getting to it

**Curator Tools** is a row in Organize (the book icon). With Livo Plus it opens the tools. Without Plus it first shows what Plus is and what stays free, with the button to buy. There is no switch to turn on in Settings: Livo Plus is the one switch. **Family**, **Receive from Family** and **Backup & Restore** are rows of their own in Organize, and are free.

## The Curator Tools screen

[Open Organize › Curator Tools](livo://organize/curator). The tools are on one screen, in four groups. Each link below opens that screen and marks the row for a moment; it never taps the row for you. These linked steps need a Livo build from 20 November 2026 or later (Settings › About › Build).

### Sources

Where photos come from and where they live: [Scan Prints](livo://organize/curator/sources/scan-prints), [Media Sources](livo://organize/curator/sources/media-sources) and [OneDrive Accounts](livo://organize/curator/sources/accounts). Add from Camera Roll and Add from OneDrive stay in the Gallery’s **≡** menu, under **Add Photos**.

### Safekeeping

[Backup & Restore](livo://organize/backup), with the date of your last backup under its name. It is free, and is a row of its own in Organize. Check a backup drive is inside it. See [Backing up and restoring](/guide/backup-and-restore.html).

### Family

[Family](livo://organize/family) shows what the family shared, with a count of what is new. [Receive from Family](livo://how/receive-family) adds it to your Gallery. Both are free, and are rows of their own in Organize. [Family Share](livo://organize/curator/family/setup) sets up your own sharing, and is part of Livo Plus. Share to Family itself is in every share sheet, for the photos you have selected. See [Family Share](/guide/family-share.html).

### Clean up and review

Each one looks first and asks before it changes anything.

- [Duplicate Check](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/duplicates) reports the photos Livo has more than once, with a button to clean them up.
- [Fix Dates from File Names](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/fix-dates) re-reads file names to correct dates already in Livo.
- [Earliest Dates](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/earliest-dates) asks OneDrive again and uses the earliest date of each item.
- [Life Events clean-up](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/life-events) finds an anniversary recorded once for each person and offers to merge them, and finds Life Events that are missing their person.
- [Find Missing Photos](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/find-missing) scans for photos whose original is in neither Photos nor OneDrive, then looks for them. **Automatic** links only the exact same file (same size and the same first 64 KB) and shows what it found before it changes anything; **Manual** goes through the photos one at a time, Livo’s copy beside each candidate. Story, tags and people always stay. Livo looks in Photos and in every signed-in OneDrive account. A photo found only in the other place is switched to it, and the old link is kept as a spare. Manual is for Camera Roll photos; OneDrive photos are matched automatically.
- [Fix Live Photo Links](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/live-links) clears a Live Photo link from a OneDrive photo that should never have had one (a LIVE badge playing the wrong clip).
- [Read Apple’s Keywords and Ratings](livo://organize/curator/cleanup/apple-keywords) adds what you set in Photos to the Camera Roll photos already in Livo: keywords become tags, a caption becomes the story where Livo has none, and four or five stars become a thumbs-up. Nothing in Photos is changed.
