## Two versions, one app

**Livo** is free, with no limits, and it is the whole loop: bring in your Camera Roll, name the people, name the event and add a line of story, make a Memory, seal a capsule, pass it on, make a gift. It works with your Camera Roll and iCloud, keeps copies in iCloud Drive and Files, and sends by AirDrop, Messages and Mail. Nothing to sign up for, and no limit on how much you make.

**Curator** is for the person who maintains the family's photos: who has the shoebox of prints to scan, who has photos in many formats from many years, cameras, phones, drives, cards and cloud accounts, and who is trying to bring all of it into one place, name it, and make it available to the family for generations. Curator adds the tools for that. It is part of **Livo Plus**, the one purchase, shared with your Apple family, never a subscription; see [Free, and Livo Plus](/guide/livo-and-livo-plus.html) for the whole plan, including what Plus adds for giving.

## What Curator adds

- **Scan Prints**: printed photos in with the camera, a box at a time, with the words on them read for search. See [Scan Prints](/guide/scan-prints.html).
- **OneDrive**: photos from OneDrive folders, backups copied off the phone, a second copy of every capsule in your own OneDrive, and capsules sent as links. See [OneDrive and your accounts](/guide/onedrive-and-your-accounts.html).
- **Family Share** and the Family Library: what each person in the family shares from their own OneDrive, and what they share back. See [Family Share](/guide/family-share.html).
- **Collections**: a fourth surface with rails for People, Places, Events, Life Events, Memories, Pinned, Saved searches, Buckets and Family.
- **Buckets** under Organize: a shelf for photos you want out of sight but not gone (a burst's thirty near-identical shots, the photos from a past relationship you are not ready to see every day). See [People, Places, Life Events, Tags and Buckets](/guide/people-places-and-more.html).
- **More Kinds**: Reference and Saved images beside Selfie, Screenshot and Scan, with text recognition on them and **Read text again** in a photo's Info.
- **More marks**: thumbs-down and Hide Thumbs Down, Picks, To Review.
- **Deeper search**: saved searches, pinned searches, and the longer grammar (commas, "and", the Story pill).
- **Housekeeping**: Media Sources, Where my photos are, Send to Photos, Check a backup drive.
- The **full selection bar** in the Gallery, with every action a tap away.

## Nothing is hidden either way

Curator never hides data. Everything a Curator tool made stays readable when Curator is off: Buckets as read-only lists, OneDrive photos already brought in still in the Gallery, capsules already copied to OneDrive still copied. Turning Curator off changes what is shown, never what is kept. A link in this guide to a Curator tool shows what the tool is and a one-line note that it is part of Curator, never an error.

## Turning it on

[Settings › Curator](livo://settings) is the switch, and it works with Livo Plus; without Plus, a tap on it shows what Plus is. On, Livo shows the Curator tools everywhere. Off, Livo is the essentials, with the Curator tools listed under **Organize › Curator tools**, where what they made stays readable.
