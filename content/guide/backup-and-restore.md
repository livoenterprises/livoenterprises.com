## What is in a backup

[Open Organize › Backup & Restore](livo://organize/backup). A backup holds every name, place, story, tag and face you have added, your Memories and narrations, Livo's small copies of your photos, what Livo Search has learned, your family folders list, and your settings. It does not hold the photos themselves (they stay in Photos and OneDrive) or your Time Capsules (they are files; see [Keeping capsules safe](/guide/keeping-capsules-safe.html)).

## Making one

**Create Backup Now** runs in the background; the card and Activity show the steps. Livo keeps the screen on while it works. Backups are named Livo_Backup_ with the date and time, and nothing already there is ever overwritten. The backup is saved on this phone (On My iPhone › Livo › Livo Backup) and, with **Copy new backups to OneDrive** on, copied to the private **Livo Backup** folder in your OneDrive. "Last backup: …" under the row tells you how long ago. Livo never backs up on its own and never reminds you with a pop-up.

A backup on the phone is deleted with the app, so keep one off the phone: in OneDrive, or share it to iCloud Drive.

## Restoring

Tap a backup in the list — on the phone, or under **Backups in OneDrive** — and **Restore**. Restoring replaces what Livo has now (your photos' records, people, places, tags, stories, Memories and learned search data) with what the backup holds; this cannot be undone. It does not touch your Time Capsule files, your OneDrive, what your family already shared with you, or your Camera Roll. Swiping a backup on the phone to delete it asks first; a copy in OneDrive stays. On the same phone, every photo links back at once.

## A new phone

Install Livo, allow access to Photos, and restore the backup. Livo finds each photo in the new phone's library by its iCloud identity, then by its content, and re-links it; a photo it cannot find keeps its record and small copy, and is counted ("N photos not found on this phone"). Sign in to OneDrive again for OneDrive photos. Copy your Time Capsules from the old phone's TimeCapsules folder, or from OneDrive › Livo Vault.
