## Choosing a Moment

Tap the calendar at the top left to see your Moments, newest first, and pick one. The ‹ and › arrows beside the date move to the earlier and the later Moment. Tap **All Moments** to see the whole library at once (a big library takes a few seconds; the card says so); picking a Moment turns it off again. When you leave the Gallery for another surface and come back, it is where you left it, filter and all.

## The Moment header

Beside the date are two small icons. The **tag** opens Tag the Moment, where you say who is in the photos, what the event was and where it happened. The **face** opens Name the Faces, which shows every face Livo found in the Moment so you can name them. See [Tag the Moment](/guide/tag-the-moment.html) and [Naming the faces](/guide/naming-the-faces.html). The Moment's menu also offers **Make a Memory of these**.

## Marked photos

Swipe a photo up to mark it (a thumbs up). The **N Marked** pill shows how many marked photos the library has; tap it to see only them. Marked photos are the set a new Memory is made from; see [Making a Memory](/guide/making-a-memory.html).

## Selecting photos

Tap a photo to select it; keep dragging to select more. A row appears under the header: **Select All**, **Clear**, **+ Event** (put the selection in an event), **Make a Memory** and **Share**. The bar at the bottom offers **Tag** (people, places and tags on all of them at once), **Info** (one photo), **Delete** (which asks first and moves the photos to the Trash), and **…** for the rest: Rotate, Set Date, Stitch for videos, Find Moment, and the Curator tools when Curator is on.

## Opening a photo

Tap a selected photo, or double-tap any photo, to see it full screen. Swipe between photos, pinch to zoom, pull down to close. A LIVE pill plays a Live Photo. The buttons under a photo include Info, Rotate, Edit Photo and, on a video, Edit Segments; see [Editing photos and videos](/guide/editing-photos-and-videos.html). The tag icon at the top opens the Details strip for that one photo. If the full-size picture cannot be shown, the caption under it says why; see [When something looks wrong](/guide/when-something-looks-wrong.html).

## The badges on a photo

A small mark in the corner tells you what a photo is: video, LIVE, stitched, used in a Memory, or **Aa** when Livo has read text on it. Curator adds marks for a scanned print and a photo in a Bucket.
