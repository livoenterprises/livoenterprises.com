## Free, forever

Everything you need to name your photos, make something from them, keep it safe and pass it on. No account, no sign-in, no counts.

- **Bring in your photos and videos** from the Camera Roll and iCloud Photos, with the duplicate clean-up. Your photos stay where they are.
- **Moments, the Gallery, Filter and Livo Search.** Search by a name, a place, an event, a date, or words Livo has read on screenshots and saved pictures.
- **Tag the Moment, Name the Faces, Edit Tags.** Who is in a day's photos, what the event was, where it happened; the faces one by one with the photo peek, the clean-up of tags, the recent names, Always offer.
- **People, Places, Events, Life Events and Tags**, with their lists under Organize. A birthday or a wedding date on a person is what makes "Emma's 5th birthday" and "when Dad was my age" searchable. The Tags list renames, merges and deletes a tag across every photo, so a typo on forty photos is one fix.
- **Stories and tags on photos**, written into the files themselves.
- **Edit Photo and Edit Segments.** Crop, rotate and straighten a photo; trim or split a video. Always as a copy; the original is never touched.
- **Memories**, as many as you like: make, edit, narrate in your own voice, reorder, combine. **Scrapbook pages and Collage pages**, Save page to Photos, and **Share as Video** for anyone, anywhere.
- **Time Capsules**: seal, look inside, Keep a copy in iCloud Drive or any folder in Files, the weekly checks and repairs, Keepsakes, Bring in, Export, Share with Livo. The page inside opens in any browser.
- **Make a Gift**: the dedication, the video Livo makes for you (with a small "with Livo" line at the end), the whole sealed gift, the folder of photos for a frame or a TV, plain print-size photos, and sending by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files.
- **Receiving, always**: opening a capsule or a gift someone sent you, Watch, Add to My Livo, Save to Photos as an album. Nobody has to buy anything to receive what you made.
- **Backup & Restore** to this phone and to Files or iCloud Drive; **Activity**, what Livo is doing in the background, with Pause and Stop; the **Trash**; Getting Started, this Guide, Settings and all five palettes.

## Livo Plus, one purchase

Made once, shared with your Apple family, never a subscription. It adds three kinds of thing.

**For giving** — the finished forms of a gift, for the people who like something in the hand or on the wall:

- **The gift book**: an 8 × 8 inch PDF with the dedication on the cover, a page per photo with the names under each, and the people at the back; plus the same pages as pictures, for print services that want images. The PDF is sealed into the gift, so whoever receives it can print it too.
- **The collage poster**, 16 × 20, for a frame or a canvas.
- **Print-size photos with the names strip**, "Anna and Laurie · November 2019" under each, so nobody has to write on the back.
- **Order prints** from Memory Lane: a book from any Memories, Scrapbook pages and Collage pages, in your order.
- **Give it again**: the whole "Your gift is ready" screen for a gift you already made (the free rows in it work for everyone).
- **Gift videos without the "with Livo" line.**

**For creating** — two things Photos cannot do, nice when making and not needed to name, seal or give:

- **Stitch videos**: several clips joined into one video, in your order, for a Memory or a gift.
- **Save Frame as Photo**: a still from any moment of a video, saved as a photo of its own.

**For the family's archive (Curator)** — for the one person who keeps thirty years of everyone's photos:

- **Scan Prints**: the shoebox in, with the camera, a stack of prints at a time, named as you go.
- **OneDrive**: photos from OneDrive folders brought in; backups and a copy of every capsule kept in your own OneDrive; capsules sent as links.
- **Family Share and the Family Library**: what each person shares from their own OneDrive, read-only to the others, with a catalog so the family's phones find photos by name.
- **Buckets**: a shelf for photos you want out of sight but not gone. The thirty near-identical shots of a burst, the photos from a past relationship you are not ready to see every day but would not delete forever, the paperwork photographed for a form. They leave the Gallery and search, and come back with a tap.
- **Collections**: a fourth surface with rails for People, Places, Events, Life Events, Memories, Pinned, Saved searches, Buckets and Family; **saved and pinned searches**; the **full selection bar**, with every action one tap away.
- **Reference and Saved-image Kinds**, thumbs-down and Hide Thumbs Down, Picks and To Review: the triage tools for a very large library.
- **Media Sources, Where my photos are, Send to Photos**, and **Check a backup drive**, which proves a mirror of your photos on an external drive is complete.

## The rules, either way

- **Nothing already made is ever locked.** Without Plus, everything you have made stays yours to see, edit and export, and what the Curator tools made (Buckets, OneDrive copies) stays readable with Curator off.
- **Tools, never a clock or a count.** No trial that expires, no limit that fills up. Plus unlocks tools, once.
- **One product.** Livo Plus is the only purchase. Curator is the name of its archive tools, and the switch for them is in Settings.
- **Restore Purchases** is under Settings › About, for a new phone or a family member's.

## Why it is drawn this way

Everything that makes a gift worth sending, and everything needed to open one, is free, because a gift that arrives is how Livo reaches the next family. Naming is always free, because names and stories are the point. The finished forms of a gift, the two extras for creating, and the tools of the person who keeps thirty years of everyone's photos are where a one-time purchase is fair.
